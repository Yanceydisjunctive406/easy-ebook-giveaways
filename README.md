## 🔑 Initial Configuration

The software requires a few steps to prepare for your first giveaway.

*   **Sign In:** Open the app and create an account. Choose a strong password to protect your reader data.
*   **Email Setup:** Connect your email marketing provider. This allows the software to add your new subscribers to the correct lists.
*   **Upload Your Book:** Drag and drop your ebook files into the storage area. The software supports standard formats like EPUB and PDF.
*   **Set Giveaway Rules:** Define the landing page title, a brief description, and the book cover image for your specific giveaway.
*   **Test the Flow:** Use the Test function to send a download link to your own email address. Confirm that the file arrives correctly.

## 📈 Running Your First Giveaway

Once you configure the settings, you can start gathering emails.

1.  Navigate to the Giveaways tab in your dashboard.
2.  Select Create New to start a campaign.
3.  Enter the name of your book.
4.  Copy the generated link provided by the tool.
5.  Share this link on your author website, social media profiles, or newsletter.
6.  Monitor your progress under the Analytics tab. You see real-time updates as readers sign up and download your files.

## ⚙️ Managing Files and Emails

The software automates the most time-consuming parts of being an author.

*   **File Security:** The system keeps your digital files secure. Only people who provide their email address gain access to the download link.
*   **Subscriber Sync:** The software syncs with your email provider every hour. You do not need to move data manually or export files.
*   **Duplicate Prevention:** The tool recognizes previous subscribers and prevents them from signing up for the same ebook multiple times.
*   **Engagement Tracking:** Identify which giveaways perform best. Use this data to change your book covers or descriptions to get more interest.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a server to run this?**
No. This desktop application manages the connection to your cloud storage and email provider for you.

**Can I run multiple giveaways at once?**
Yes. You can manage several campaigns at the same time using the dashboard.

**What happens if I lose my internet connection?**
The software stores your data locally. It synchronizes your changes once you return to the internet.

**Does this software store my email list locally?**
It acts as a bridge. The tool sends data to your chosen email provider. Your primary list remains with your email service provider for safe keeping.

**How do I update the software?**
The program checks for updates upon launch. If a new version exists, it prompts you to download and apply the improvements.
